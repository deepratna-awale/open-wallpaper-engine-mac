These files are derived from Wallpaper Engine (Kristjan Skutta / Wallpaper Engine Team).

Shaders have been translated from GLSL to Metal Shading Language; effect manifests, materials,
textures, models, particle definitions and the SceneScript runtime are included in their original
form. Editor preview art and the original GLSL sources have been omitted.

The files in locale/ are Wallpaper Engine's UI strings (locale/ui_*.json of an install), included
unchanged; they translate the localisation keys wallpapers use as property labels.

The fonts in fonts/ are Wallpaper Engine's built-in fonts. Each keeps its own licence; the
licence files shipped next to them (e.g. "SIL Open Font License.txt") are included unchanged.

Wallpaper Engine: https://www.wallpaperengine.io
