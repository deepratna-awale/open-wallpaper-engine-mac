Wallpaper Engine attribution
============================

This is the one place where Open Wallpaper Engine credits Wallpaper Engine.

Open Wallpaper Engine is an independent, open-source macOS app. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by or connected to Wallpaper Engine or its developers. "Wallpaper Engine" is the name
of a commercial application by Kristjan Skutta / Wallpaper Engine Team, sold on Steam:
https://www.wallpaperengine.io

Neither this repository nor the app contains any of Wallpaper Engine's files. Scene wallpapers
need Wallpaper Engine's built-in effects, materials, models, particles, GLSL shaders, SceneScript
runtime, compatibility patches, fonts and UI strings; the app takes them from the user's own copy
of Wallpaper Engine on Steam:

- Settings > Assets downloads only those files and Wallpaper Engine's default wallpapers from the
  user's copy, through the app's Steam helper and the Steam account the user signed in with (it
  must own Wallpaper Engine), into the user's Wallpaper Storage folder; or
- the user chooses the folder of an existing Wallpaper Engine install, which is read in place.

The fonts keep their own licences; their licence files (e.g. "SIL Open Font License.txt") come
with them.

Wallpapers themselves belong to their Steam Workshop authors and are not part of this project.
